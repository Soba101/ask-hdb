Heights are illustrative, from floor counts. Sun geometry is illustrative, not measured heat. The 3D light shades each wall by its direction but does not cast shadows onto other buildings. Shadows drawn on the ground are illustrative (ground shadows only), using the convex hull of each footprint; this overestimates L-shaped and courtyard blocks. No trees or clouds; overlaps may look darker. Shadows appear at zoom 15 or closer, for up to 600 buildings in view (dense areas may miss some), with lengths capped at 300 m and hidden below 10° sun altitude. Exposure colours and direct-sun hours ignore shade from other blocks. In Type mode, grey to yellow shows increasing exposure on the sunnier long facade. Facing is approximate per block, not per unit.
