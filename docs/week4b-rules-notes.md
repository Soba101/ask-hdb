## Conflicts between pages

1. Supply split for second-timers: [OCT24-C] fn 2 says "at least 95% ... first-timers, and up to 5% ... second-timers". [FEB26-B] and [JUN26-B] say 90% / 10%. The quota appears to have changed between Oct 2024 and Feb 2026.
2. Downpayment vs LTV: the Jun 2026 annex says 10% (HDB loan) or 20% (FI loan) downpayment at Agreement for Lease, but the same annex says both LTV limits are 75%. That implies a total of 25%, so the 10%/20% is likely only the portion due at Agreement for Lease, with the rest at key collection. This is inferred and not stated.
3. Deferred income assessment eligibility: [DIA-A] says "Both parties of a couple must" be students or NSFs. A search summary said "one party". The PDF is authoritative.
4. 2026 schedule: the Jan 2026 article planned Feb, Jun and Oct exercises. The homes.hdb.gov.sg homepage says November 2026 (per search summary).
5. Maximum HDB loan tenure: the calculator allows 1-25 years. The financial statements mention up to 30 years.
