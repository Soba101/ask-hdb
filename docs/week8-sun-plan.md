# Week 8-3: 3D appears when you tilt, and the sun is live

## Goal
1. The 3D buildings switch on by themselves when the user tilts the map, and the flat map returns when the map is level again.
2. The sun is "real time": a Now button (Singapore time), a play button that runs the day, and shadows that move with it.
3. Parts of a block are lit or dark: each wall is shaded by its own direction, and buildings cast shadows on the ground. Shadows do not fall on other buildings' walls or roofs (a flat layer under the extrusions cannot do that); the label says "ground shadows only".

## Prior art
ShadeMap (shademap.app, Ted Piotrowski) and `mapbox-gl-shadow-simulator` render shade from a height raster on the GPU. They are built for terrain and need either a licence or a DEM source, so they are not a fit for HDB footprints. We do the same idea analytically on the outlines we already ship.

## Design
- **Auto 3D.** Split the toggle into `setEnabled(on, {ease})`. Only `pitchend` events with `originalEvent` (user gestures) trigger auto mode: pitch above 10 degrees calls `setEnabled(true, {ease:false})`, pitch below 3 degrees calls `setEnabled(false, {ease:false})`. Auto paths never call `easeTo` and never change `oldPitch`; the manual toggle's own `easeTo` calls fire `pitchend` without `originalEvent`, so they cannot loop. A second enable while `/map/buildings` is loading is ignored; after one failed load, tilting stops retrying (the button shows the error). The 3D button stays as a manual override and is the way to 3D on phones without a tilt gesture.
- **Per-wall shading.** The extrusion layer already lights each wall via `map.setLight`. Raise light intensity and colour contrast so a wall facing away from the sun is visibly darker than one facing it, even in Type mode. The per-building exposure colour (grey to yellow) stays as an option.
- **Shadows** (`src/ask_hdb/api/static/shadows.js`). Use `sun.azimuth` (compass degrees from north, as in `ask_hdb/sun.py`), not the `setLight` position, which has a +180 offset. For a building of height h and sun altitude alt: L = min(h / tan(alt), 300 m); offset east = -sin(az)*L metres, north = -cos(az)*L metres; convert to degrees with lon = east/(111320*cos(lat)) and lat = north/110540. Shadow polygon = convex hull of the footprint vertices plus the same vertices shifted by the offset, one hull per polygon of a MultiPolygon (never across parts). No shadow when altitude is below 10 degrees. Select buildings in the viewport widened on the sun's side by 300 m, at zoom 15 or more, at most about 600. One `setData` per animation frame for a single source, skipping a frame while the previous one is still running (no partial updates, so no mixed times). Layer: fill, added with `beforeId: "blocks"` so it sits above the map but below the point circles and the extrusions. Hidden and cleared when 3D is off.
- **Real time.** "Now" uses UTC+8 (not the browser zone), rounds down to the quarter hour and clamps to the slider range 06:00-19:30; outside it shows "The sun is down." If the date differs it goes through `loadDay` and its stale-response guard before moving the slider. Play advances one 15-minute step per animation tick (about 8 s for the whole slider range), stops at 19:30 (no wrap), and stops on manual input (slider, date, or the 3D toggle; not map panning) or when the tab is hidden. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables play.
- **Honest labels, in the right place.** The Python text (`sun.py` LIMITS, `facade_day`, direct-sun hours) still says shade from other blocks is ignored, and stays unchanged because those numbers still ignore it. The 3D panel text and `docs/week7-3d.md` get the new wording: shadows are illustrative, ground shadows only, convex hull of each footprint (overestimates L-shaped and courtyard blocks), no trees or clouds, overlaps may look darker, heights from floor counts, and "the light does not cast building shadows".
- No Python API, agent, tool or prompt change; no new dependency; no new network call; no `innerHTML`.

## Contract that must not change
All ids and behaviours listed in docs/week8-plan.md, plus the mobile Layers overlay (docs: mobile PR), `wallExposure` parity with `ask_hdb/sun.py`, the existing sun tests, and the stale-response guards.

## Done when

- Checked in a browser: tilt turns 3D on, a block shows a lit and a dark wall, shadows move with the slider, desktop and 375px.
